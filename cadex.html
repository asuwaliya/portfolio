CADEX Legal — moving accounts

A legal workflow app for CADEX, a commercial collection agency. It gives collectors, legal admins, specialists, managers and attorneys one shared view of every account on its way to legal — with timelines, actions, documents and calls in one place.

01 / OVERVIEW

One account,
five roles.

When third-party collection efforts are exhausted, an account moves to legal — and passes through many hands before it reaches an attorney.

The brief

Define the information architecture and user flows for every role in CADEX’s legal process, build a design system, and design the core screens of the legal web app.

Scope of work

Role-based IA

User flows

Module definition

Design system

UI design

Edge cases

Every account carries a story — collection efforts, documents, emails and calls. The legal app keeps that story intact as the account changes hands.

Accounts are recommended for legal (RFL), reviewed by a legal manager, backed up with client documents by a legal admin, escalated to a specialist when needed and finally placed with an attorney who recommends a suit — each step with its own status, deadlines and notifications.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem
statement.

A legal account is high-stakes: deadlines, statutes of limitation and client approvals all matter. Nothing can fall through the cracks between roles.

Problem statement

How might we give five different legal roles one shared,
trustworthy view of an account — and the right next action?

Each role needs different data and permissions, yet all of them act on the same account and its history.

BUSINESS GOALS

Move accounts to legal faster

Reduce hand-off delays between collection and legal, automate follow-ups and keep clients informed.

USER GOALS

Know what to do next

See an account’s full history, status and deadlines at a glance, and take action without switching tools.

CONSTRAINTS

What we worked within

Login via Cadex ID or Microsoft, client approval rules, statute-of-limitation checks and a LiveVox calling integration.

03 / USERS

Designing for
five roles.

We mapped responsibilities role by role before drawing a single screen — each role became its own IA and set of flows.

COLLECTOR

Legal Collector

Handles accounts over $1,000 for recovery, calls debtors through LiveVox and hands accounts to legal with a summary of efforts.

LEGAL ADMIN

Legal Admin

Receives RFL accounts, gathers backup documents from clients and sends accounts for pre-legal review.

SPECIALIST

Legal Specialist

Steps in when documents are late or an attorney doesn’t respond, and manages client authorisation for suits.

MANAGER

Legal Manager

Reviews accounts recommended for legal and takes over escalations when specialists can’t reach clients.

ATTORNEY

Attorney

Acknowledges placed accounts, uses document templates and makes suit recommendations within set timeframes.

06 / FINAL DESIGNS

The final
experience.

A calm, high-contrast interface: a black header for orientation, CADEX green for action, and colour-coded status chips that tell each role where an account stands.

Account detail · legal manager view

FEATURE 01

Every account, sorted by status

The account list groups accounts by legal status — LMR, LNR, LBS — with desk filters, attorney firm and total due visible without opening a record.

Problem

Roles couldn’t tell which accounts needed them

Decision

Status tabs and chips, plus a desk filter per role

FEATURE 02

The whole story on one timeline

Account history shows every step — legal review, backup requested, follow-up mails — with who did it, when and from which desk.

Problem

Context was lost every time an account changed hands

Decision

A single, filterable timeline with expandable notes

FEATURE 03

A guided pre-legal review

The Review Legal form walks the legal admin through statute of limitations, contact attempts, documents, disputes and payments — then saves as draft or continues.

Problem

Pre-legal checks were long, manual and error-prone

Decision

Step-by-step yes / no questions that reveal only relevant fields

FEATURE 04

Calls without leaving the account

The Call tab pairs call history with a phone box, and connects to LiveVox so collectors can dial, record and transfer from the account itself.

Problem

Collectors switched between tools to call debtors

Decision

LiveVox integrated into the account’s Call tab

Key moments & states

Add Action — move an account to the next stage with status, desk and note

Instant confirmation when an account is escalated to a specialist

Live call controls — record, mute, hold, transfer and end

07 / REFLECTION

What I
learned.

What worked, what I’d push further, and what comes next.

LEARNING 01

Map roles before screens

Writing one IA per role exposed overlaps and gaps early — and made the shared account detail page far easier to design.

LEARNING 02

Edge cases are the product

Late documents, unresponsive attorneys and client declines shaped the flows as much as the happy path did.

LEARNING 03

Status is the interface

Consistent status codes and colours (RFL, LMR, LBR, LBS…) let every role read an account in seconds.
