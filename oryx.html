01 / OVERVIEW

One app,
two sides of a sale.

ORYX sells to retail shops through a field sales team. The app connects both sides — the salesman in the field and the shop owner behind the counter.

The brief

Design a mobile app for two roles — salesman and shop owner — covering onboarding, catalogue and ordering, quotations, finance, service requests, support and account management.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem
statement.

B2B ordering usually means phone calls, paper catalogues and spreadsheets. The app had to make it faster without losing the human sales relationship.

Problem statement

How might we let salesmen and shop owners order, quote and
manage accounts from a phone — quickly and accurately?

Large catalogues, many SKUs, multiple branches per shop, credit limits and delivery logistics all need to fit on a 390px screen.

BUSINESS GOALS

Sell more, with less friction

Grow orders per shop, speed up onboarding, and give the sales team live visibility of customers and targets.

USER GOALS

Find, order and track fast

Find the right product in seconds, place or quote an order on the spot, and always know credit and delivery status.

CONSTRAINTS

What we worked within

Two roles with different permissions, multi-branch shops, SAR pricing per sqm or unit, and pickup / split delivery.

07 / FINAL DESIGNS

The final
experience.

A clean, monochrome UI that lets product imagery and numbers do the talking — consistent cards, clear actions and a bottom tab bar per role.

Main screens

FEATURE 01

Find any product in seconds

Search the catalogue by name or SKU, snap a photo of a tile to search by image, or scan a QR code — then filter by size, colour, availability and warehouse.

Problem

Huge catalogues of similar-looking tiles

Decision

Three search modes plus rich filters

FEATURE 02

Finance at a glance

Wallet, incentives, invoices, transactions and credit limit sit on the owner’s home, so they always know what they can spend and what’s due.

Problem

Credit and payments lived offline

Decision

Finance cards on the dashboard with drill-down screens

FEATURE 03

Onboard a shop on the spot

Salesmen onboard new shops, upload documents and see a full customer profile — orders, quotations, branches and requests — before every visit.

Problem

Onboarding needed paperwork and follow-ups

Decision

Guided onboarding and a 360° customer detail view

FEATURE 04

Branches & staff, managed

Shop owners add branches and staff, assign roles and see each branch’s orders, quotations and service requests.

Problem

Multi-branch shops were hard to manage

Decision

Branch listing, staff management and per-branch overviews

FEATURE 05

Plan pickups & split deliveries

A pickup trip planner lets owners split an order across trucks and dates, then submit the plan for confirmation.

Problem

Large orders don’t fit one delivery

Decision

Truck plan and split delivery built into order flow

08 / REFLECTION

What I
learned.

What worked, what I’d push further, and what comes next.

LEARNING 01

Shared patterns, two roles

Designing catalogue, cart and quotation once and reusing them for both roles kept 413 screens consistent.

LEARNING 02

Prototype the wireframes

Wiring wireframes into clickable flows caught logic gaps early — before visual design.

LEARNING 03

Search is the product

For thousands of similar tiles, image and QR search mattered more than any layout tweak.

What’s next

→

Apply the ORYX brand colour system on top of the monochrome UI

→

Usability test search-by-image and split delivery with shop owners

→

Track order frequency per shop and onboarding time after launch
