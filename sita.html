02 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem
statement.

Building a custom tour involves dozens of decisions and suppliers. The tool had to keep complex data manageable — and the price always visible.

Problem statement

How might we help operations teams turn a travel query into
an accurate, priced proposal — without losing track of details?

Every query has customers, source markets, room types, travellers, flights and many products — and the price changes with every edit.

BUSINESS GOALS

Respond to enquiries faster

Shorten the time from query to proposal, reduce pricing errors and keep every query assigned and tracked.

USER GOALS

Build tours with confidence

See query status at a glance, add products quickly and always know the proposal cost.

CONSTRAINTS

What we worked within

Tailor-made and series tours, seasons and departures, multiple versions, validation and supervisor review.

07 / FINAL DESIGNS

The final
experience.

A clean, navy-and-blue interface where queries, products and prices are always one glance away.

Tour summary · version view

FEATURE 01

Every query, tracked by status

Status tabs (Recent, Unassigned, Draft, In progress, Submitted, Confirmed), version badges and one-click assignment keep a large pipeline under control.

Problem

Unassigned or stalled queries were easy to miss

Decision

Status tabs with counts, highlighted “Assign” actions and version history

FEATURE 02

Traveller & stay details, structured

Rooms, persons, team leaders, dates and arrival/departure details are captured in clear accordion sections before building begins.

Problem

Long forms and repeated data entry

Decision

Grouped accordions with pre-filled values and clear progress

FEATURE 03

An itinerary builder with real imagery

Hotels, activities and monuments are added from rich cards with images, so executives build tours they can picture — and sell.

Problem

Text-only product lists made choices slow

Decision

Image-led product cards inside a right-hand drawer

FEATURE 04

Pricing you can trust

A collapsible price breakdown and proposal cost panel update as products change, with versions to compare options side by side.

Problem

Price changes were hard to follow

Decision

Always-visible proposal cost, detailed breakdown and versions

FEATURE 05

Series tours, seasons & departures

Series queries get dedicated seasons and departure planning, so recurring tours are priced and scheduled in one view.

Problem

Series tours needed different logic from tailor-made

Decision

A seasons & departures view built for repeating tours

18 screens, one system

09 / REFLECTION

What I
learned.

What worked, what I’d push further, and what comes next.

LEARNING 01

Wireframe the logic first

Agreeing where drawers, products and pricing live in low-fidelity saved rework in a very data-heavy UI.

LEARNING 02

Keep the price in view

An always-visible proposal cost turned pricing from a final step into a live guide while building.

LEARNING 03

Test the real tasks

Scenario-based testing on query creation and traveller details focused feedback on the flows used every day.
